Board guide
  • Scroll over the board to move through the current history.
  • Right-click a square or drag to annotate; hold Ctrl for red annotations.
  • Green squares show the best move: full colour from depth 46, paler the shallower the analysis.
  • Purple squares show live analysis, shown while it is deeper than the saved result and saved once it is deeper than the saved Stockfish 19 analysis. It needs the engine bridge or the browser engine; turn it on or off with the button above the board.
  • The bar left of the board shows the evaluation, also while an analysis is running.

      Engine bridge: not connected
      A 99 MB download the first time (the browser keeps it), and several times slower than the engine bridge. It only runs live analysis: analysing to a chosen depth needs the bridge. Stockfish is free software under the GPLv3; this build is stockfish.js 19, from Stockfish.

      Stockfish runs on your own computer. A small helper program, the engine bridge, starts it for this site and downloads the official Stockfish 19 for you. Nothing is installed system-wide.

      Your browser will ask once whether this site may access other apps and services on this device. Choose Allow: that is how this page reaches the bridge, and the bridge only answers this site.

      The first command installs and starts the bridge. Later, start it from the "My Chess DB Bridge" launcher it adds (the Start menu on Windows), or with the second command. This panel closes by itself once the bridge is running.

      Safari cannot connect to the engine bridge. Saved moves work here, but to analyse with Stockfish open this site in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

        Puzzle list
          Settings and data Lichess's own computer analysis searches every position to 1,000,000 nodes. "Quicker" is about three times as fast and finds a little less.
          Everything is kept in this browser only: the games, the puzzles and how you solved them. Export moves them to another browser or device (a phone, for instance, which cannot run the engine bridge but can solve the puzzles).
          How the puzzles are made
          • Give your chess.com and/or Lichess username: the puzzles of both are shown together, each marked with its site.
          • Update asks the engine bridge on your computer to fetch your rated rapid games and analyse the ones in which your rating was at least 80% of your current rapid rating (for each site its own rating), newest first, until all of them are done. It goes on by itself while the bridge runs, also with this tab closed; the puzzles it found show up here the next time. When your rating changes, Update re-checks the games already fetched and analyses the ones that now count.
          • Each game is analysed by Stockfish 19 the way Lichess's computer analysis does it (1,000,000 nodes per position). A Lichess game that Lichess has already analysed uses Lichess's own evaluations.
          • Puzzles are found the way Lichess finds its puzzles: where your opponent's move gave you a winning chance or a mate, and the winning line is one where each of your moves is clearly the only good one.
          • Lichess only makes puzzles where the opponent has just blundered. So where your move was a mistake or a blunder, the position is also a puzzle when its best move was the only good one: every other move would have been a blunder too.
          • A puzzle is kept only if you missed it in the game.
          • A puzzle solved at the first try comes back after 1, 3, then 7 days; solved a fourth time in a row, it leaves the list. A wrong move or the solution sends it back to the start, and it keeps coming back until you solve it.
